Cryptography is the practice and study of using mathematical algorithms and
protocols to secure information against unauthorized access and adversarial
behavior.

## Core Principles

Modern cryptography relies on four foundational security pillars:

- **Confidentiality**: Ensuring only authorized recipients can read the message.
- **Integrity**: Guaranteeing the data has not been altered or tampered with in
  transit or storage.
- **Authentication**: Verifying the identity of the communicating parties or the
  origin of the data.
- **Non-repudiation**: Preventing a sender from denying they sent a message.

## Common Applications

- **Secure Web Browsing**: Protecting data in transit via HTTPS, SSL, and TLS.
- **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)**: Securing private messages and communications.
- **Cryptocurrencies & Blockchains**: Ensuring transaction validity and network
  security.
- **Digital Signatures & Authentication**: Confirming user identities and
  software authenticity.
